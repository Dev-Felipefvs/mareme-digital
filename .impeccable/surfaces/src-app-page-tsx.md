# Home (/) — surface brief

Scope: the MAREME Digital home, Persuade mode. The audience is local business owners, often arriving from a demo link. The action is a WhatsApp conversation. Proof is the 6 real demo sites. Constraints: the black + lime palette is pinned; no invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the agency category standard played straight (the user chose canon), at the craft level of Linear, Vercel and Locomotive. It refuses the AI-agency tics the old page wore: italic serif accents, glowing blurred blobs, film grain, and floating rounded cards with a spotlight.
OWN-WORLD: an ink #101010 ground. One grotesk family (Geist) set tight for display, with Geist Mono for indices and labels. Content sits on a hairline 1px grid of cells (rgb 250 250 247 / .1) instead of floating cards, with 8px radii on media and buttons. Lime #C5F277 appears only on the primary action and on live/active states.
STORY: a business owner sees real sites MAREME built, learns that their own site is built before they sign, and opens WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: nav bar (logo left, text links, small lime CTA). The left 7 columns hold the headline "Seu próximo cliente pode estar procurando por você agora." at about 70–78px (sized so each line holds on one row), the subcopy, the lime primary button and a ghost secondary, then the demo-before-sign line at 20px in paper with a lime live dot (sans, not mono: craft floor refuses mono as costume). The right 5 columns hold a browser frame with a real demo site (Tânia), with a phone capture overlapping. No stats row (craft floor: hero-metric template refused).
FORM: canon, the category standard (the standing exit chosen by the user over an assigned "Fachada Acesa" world). seed: 2a8dcf0d0bdff26f
Signature interaction: the hero browser frame cycles through the real demo sites with a crossfade and a mono URL ticker. Motion grammar: short 0.6–0.9s expo-out entrances; no scroll-jacking.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Update 2026-11-12: user-pinned direction (supersedes the canon contract above)
The user asked to rebuild the whole site as a conversion landing page, with the Behance project "Página de Vendas — Desafio O Grande Salto" as the main reference. This is pinned by the user and beats the roll. The reference sets the composition only; no texts, logos or numbers were copied.
- Ground: ink #101010 with lime radial glows. The founder photo gets a lime duotone (grayscale multiplied by lime).
- Type: Archivo with its width axis, extra-bold and slightly wide for display.
- CTAs: lime blocks in caps that go to WhatsApp.
- Sections, in order: hero (promise with lime highlight, 3 checks, big CTA, stacked "site published" cards of real projects); demo-before-sign proof (tilted phones + 3 steps); "O que você recebe" (3 zigzag cards with lime connectors + CTA); real sites grid; "Conheça quem vai criar o seu site: Emerson" (photo + thumbs); final caps CTA band; slim footer.
- Removed from the page: Agency, Services, Process and the contact form.
- The canon version is kept on branch redesign-canon.
