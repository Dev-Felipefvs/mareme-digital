# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Business owners and independent professionals (architects, dentists and clinics, real-estate brokers, lawyers) who are evaluating whether to hire MAREME for a website. Many arrive after Emerson has already shown them a demo of their own site, often from a WhatsApp link.

## Product Purpose
MAREME Digital is Emerson's one-person studio in Maceió/AL that builds websites: institutional sites, landing pages, portfolios, and maintenance. The site has to make a visitor trust the studio's craft and start a conversation on WhatsApp.

## Positioning
MAREME builds the client's site before the client signs: the prospect sees their own business already online as a working demo, then decides. (Confirmed by the user, 2026-11-12.)

## Operating Context
Prospects come in through Google Maps research, a WhatsApp conversation, and a demo link on GitHub Pages. Contact happens on WhatsApp (5582987797962) or Instagram @mareme.mkt. The contact form either posts to an optional endpoint or sends to WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 15 static export on GitHub Pages at mareme.com.br, Tailwind 4, GSAP and Motion.
- Content lives in `src/data/site.ts` (founder texts, contacts) and `src/data/projects.ts` (portfolio).
- No commercial e-mail yet.
- The logo is provisional: a lime M on a black square.

## Brand Commitments
- Palette is pinned (user request, 2026-11-12) to the exact palette of the Behance reference "Página de Vendas — O Grande Salto": Preto #000000, Verde Limão #52FF00, #DBFF00 and the degradê #52FF00 → #DBFF00 (vertical). Dark-green grounds #0A1300, #081100, #040A00; glows #003900 → #007E00 → #00B101; lines #1B4400; cards #1C1C1A. This replaces the earlier black + #C5F277 palette.
- The user previously rejected a custom cursor, an animated particle background, stars or dots on the service marquee, borders on the marquee, and "Cardápios digitais".
- Keep the founder's own "Sobre mim" texts and his full-body photo.

## Evidence on Hand
- Portfolio: 6 real sites, currently labelled "Projeto conceito" (prospecting demos), with desktop and mobile captures in `public/`.
  - Real figures only: 6 projects published, direct one-to-one service.
  - No testimonials, client logos, prices, or results exist. Do not fabricate them.
- Founder photos are in `public/images/`.

## Product Principles
1. Show, don't claim: the work itself is the proof.
2. Talking to Emerson is the action; every path leads to WhatsApp.
3. Never invent numbers, clients, or results.
